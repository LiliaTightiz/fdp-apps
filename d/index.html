Episode 7 · Parameterized quantum circuits

Qubits Pick the Chargers

Four EVs wait. The feeder can serve two right now. One qubit per EV. The circuit holds all 16 choices at once; a classical optimizer tunes its angles until the best choice is the most likely result.

The decision

  • Exactly 2 EVs are served now. Each served EV earns its urgency score.
  • A and B share phase L1. If both are served, the phase imbalance costs 3.
C(x) = −(5x_A + 4x_B + 3x_C + 1x_D) + 4(Σx − 2)² + 3·x_A·x_B
Greedy: A + Btop two urgency scores · cost −6
Best: A + Cfound by a full search of 16 · cost −8

One qubit per EV: 4 qubits hold 2⁴ = 16 choices. A feeder with 50 EVs would need 50 qubits for 2⁵⁰ ≈ 1.1 × 10¹⁵ choices. That growth is the reason to study quantum methods for coordination.

An honest note. Sixteen choices take a laptop less than a microsecond to check. QAOA is studied for very large decisions, where a full search is impossible. Whether it beats the best classical methods at that scale is still an open research question.

The same loop trains a quantum critic. A circuit with angles θ gives a measured output; a classical optimizer tunes θ. In QS-MARL the output is a value estimate for the joint state of the microgrid agents, with one qubit per agent.

Equations, checks and references
|ψ(γ, β)⟩ = Π_l e^(−iβ_l H_M) e^(−iγ_l C/4) |ψ₀⟩

X mixer. H_M = Σ X_i and |ψ₀⟩ = |+⟩⊗4, so all 16 choices start equal. The constraint "exactly 2" lives only in the penalty term of C.

XY mixer. H_M = Σ_ring (X_iX_j + Y_iY_j)/2 and |ψ₀⟩ is the Dicke state of the six choices with two EVs. This mixer moves weight only between valid choices, so the constraint holds by design.

objective: min over (γ, β) of ⟨ψ|C|ψ⟩

Checks. The browser statevector simulator matches a SciPy matrix exponential reference to 1.3e-14 for both mixers at p = 1, 2 and 3. Best known angles come from a multistart L-BFGS-B search inside the slider ranges.

Readout error. Each measured bit flips with the chosen probability. The bars stay exact; the white ticks show what a noisy device would report.
